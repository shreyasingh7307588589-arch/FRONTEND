Welcome to the Student Portal

R. R. Institute of Modern Technology is a college that provides quality education and helps students develop technical and practical skills. It focuses on students knowledge and career growth.

Fall Semester Course Registration Open

The college was established to provide quality technical education and better career opportunities to students. Over the years, it has focused on practical learning, technology, and students’ overall development. Enrollment for the Fall 2026 semester is now officially open. All students are advised to review their degree plans, consult with academic advisors, and complete course registration via the Registration before the August 31st deadline.

Accreditation and Recognition

R.R Group of Modern Technical is accredited by the Higher Education Commission. Its computer science and engineering programs are certified by ABET.
