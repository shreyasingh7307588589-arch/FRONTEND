About Our Institution

Our Mission Statement

R. R. Institute of Modern Technology is a college that provides quality education and helps students develop technical and practical skills. It focuses on students knowledge and career growth.

Our History

The college was established to provide quality technical education and better career opportunities to students. Over the years, it has focused on practical learning, technology, and students’ overall development.

Accreditation and Recognition

R.R Group of Modern Technical is accredited by the Higher Education Commission. Its computer science and engineering programs are certified by ABET.
